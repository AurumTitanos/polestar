- Polestar plays with a gamepad. RB + D-pad up opens the options the first
  time a gamepad is active; their new Gamepad page names the buttons (Xbox or
  PlayStation) and binds every Polestar action to a key, a button or two
  buttons held together. "Browse the guide with the gamepad" lets the D-pad
  pick a line in the guide window (A points the arrow or uses the quest item,
  X shows the details, LB / RB change steps, Y minimizes, B gives the gamepad
  back), and every Polestar window answers the D-pad, A, B, Y and LB / RB
  while it is open, with a bar of button hints. Nothing is taken from the
  game while a window is merely shown, nor in combat (ported from Ding! by
  Yendi, @moust4ki).
